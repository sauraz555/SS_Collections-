## THE PROBLEM (Why This Work Exists)

Saurav spent **$35 on Facebook Meta ads** using **Advantage Plus audience**.
Result: **100 leads, 0 conversions, 0 sales.**

**Root causes identified:**
1. Advantage Plus = unqualified cold traffic (wrong objective for this stage)
2. No WhatsApp Business = leads had nowhere to go
3. No price shown in ads = buyers dropped off
4. No urgency/offer = no reason to act
5. No follow-up system = 100 leads evaporated

---

## THE STRATEGY (What We Built)

### Fix #1 — Facebook Ads
- Kill Advantage Plus immediately
- Switch to **MESSAGES → WhatsApp** objective
- Manual targeting: Bagmati Province, age 18–45, fashion/jewellery/gifts interests
- 3 separate campaigns: Ladies (bangles), Gents (premium), Couple/Gift sets
- Rs 200/day per campaign = Rs 600/day total

### Fix #2 — WhatsApp Business
- Set up WhatsApp Business with catalogue, auto-replies, quick replies
- 6-step sales conversation script
- Reply within 5 minutes (60% chance of sale)
- Full objection bank (17 objections + responses)

### Fix #3 — Content
- Post 5–7x/week using weekly calendar
- Show price in EVERY post
- Add trust package to EVERY post: ✅ 1 Year Warranty ✅ 7 Day Exchange ✅ Free Delivery ✅ COD ✅ WhatsApp: [NUMBER]
- Use existing MP4 videos as Reels immediately

### Fix #4 — Influencers
- Barter deal: 1 free watch (Rs 675–950 cost) = 1 Reel + 2 Stories
- 15 micro-influencers identified (see Research Reports)
- Track via unique promo codes (SSHS10 format)

### Fix #5 — Intelligence
- Top 3 "competitors" are actually 1 operation (The Watch Lounge + NaviForce + SKMEI = same WhatsApp, same Lalitpur showroom)
- SS Collection's unfair advantages: Bhaktapur location (faster delivery), bangle collection (20+ SKUs), digital watch volume (19 SKUs), couple/gift niche
