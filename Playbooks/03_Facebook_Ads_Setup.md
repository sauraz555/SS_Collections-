## On Buying Followers

**Do NOT buy fake followers.**
- Facebook shows posts to a % of your followers
- Bots don't engage → Facebook marks content as poor → throttles your reach
- Your organic reach collapses permanently

**Instead:** Run a "Page Follow" ad at Rs 100/day targeting real Nepali buyers.

## Meta Pixel + Conversions API

Install immediately. Track these events:
- **Purchase** — every completed sale
- **Add to Cart (ATC)** — WhatsApp message initiated
- **ViewContent** — watch product page viewed

Without pixel data, you cannot build retargeting audiences or use lookalike audiences.

---

## Weekly Ad Review Checklist

Every 7 days:
1. Which campaign got the most WhatsApp messages?
2. Which creative had the lowest cost per message?
3. Scale the winner by 20%. Pause everything else.
4. Test 1 new creative against the winner.
5. Never change a winning ad — duplicate it and test variants.

## Ad Creative Rules (From Competitor Research)

What The Watch Lounge does that works:
1. **Video/Reel on-wrist** — Show the watch ticking on a real wrist. Not product photos.
2. **Price shown upfront** — "Under Rs X" or "Rs 999 only" in first 4 seconds.
3. **Trust package** — Warranty + exchange + free delivery. Every single ad.
4. **WhatsApp number** — Visible in every video end screen.
5. **COD** — Mentioned explicitly. Removes all payment risk for Nepali buyers.
