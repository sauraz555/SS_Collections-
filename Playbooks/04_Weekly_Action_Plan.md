## 6-Step WhatsApp Sales Formula (Quick Reference)

1. **Greet within 5 min** — "Namaste! SS Collection ma swagat cha. Hajurlai kun watch chai?"
2. **Qualify** — Ladies/gents/couple? Budget? For self or gift?
3. **Show 2–3 options only** — More than 3 = buyer confusion = no sale.
4. **Handle objection** — "Bazarma Rs 4,000 ko watch — hami direct import, Rs 1,900 matra."
5. **Urgency** — "Yo model last 3 piece cha. Aaja order garnus?"
6. **Easy close** — eSewa / Khalti / bank / COD — their choice. Confirm address.

**Speed rule:** Reply in under 5 minutes → 60% chance of sale. After 1 hour → 80% are gone.
