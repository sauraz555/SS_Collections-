💰 Nepal Price Psychology — Critical Rules

Always Use Odd Pricing
❌ Rs 900  →  ✅ Rs 899
❌ Rs 1,000 → ✅ Rs 999
❌ Rs 1,500 → ✅ Rs 1,499
❌ Rs 2,000 → ✅ Rs 1,999
❌ Rs 2,600 → ✅ Rs 2,599
Framings That Kill Price Objections
✅ "Direct import — no middleman"
✅ "Direct factory price"
✅ "Premium build, Nepal price"
✅ "Bazarma Rs 4,000 → Hamro Rs 1,499"
✅ "1 year warranty included"
✅ "Cash on delivery — try first"
Words That Never Work in Nepal
❌ "Cheap" (implies poor quality)
❌ "Low quality" angle
❌ "Replica" or "Copy"
❌ Hide price ("DM for price")
❌ "Limited time" without real deadline
❌ English-only posts to local audience

🎬 Rule: First 3 seconds determine whether your video gets watched. Film all of these with just your phone. Natural light is better than indoor light. Film the hook 5–10 times and keep the best take. These 3 hooks are confirmed high-performers from South Asian watch market data.
🥇 Hook 1 — Wrist Sparkle/Transformation (Best Performer)
What to film (0–3 sec): Bare wrist → slide watch on slowly → twist wrist to catch light.

Nepali text overlay: "यो घडी लगाएपछि स्टाइल एकदम फरक पर्छ!" (Your style completely changes after wearing this watch!)

Continue (3–15 sec): Show watch from multiple angles. Price reveal at 8 sec: "मात्र Rs [PRICE] मा" (Only Rs X). End: WhatsApp number on screen + "COD Available."

Music: Trending Nepali/Hindi beat or upbeat instrumental. Low volume.
📱 Phone tip: Hold hand steady in sunlight near a window. Slow rotate to catch light sparkle on dial/strap. Film in 1080p. If bangle watch, this hook is 10x more powerful — the sparkle is irresistible.
🥈 Hook 2 — Price Pop Reveal (Drives Immediate Clicks)
What to film (0–3 sec): Close-up of watch face in hand → flip over showing the dial → price text explodes on screen.

On-screen text sequence: "Bazarma Rs 4,000+ 🚫" (cross out) → "Hamro Price: Rs 999 ✅"

Nepali voice/text: "मात्र Rs ९९९ मा premium look!" (Premium look for only Rs 999!)

Continue: Show 3 colour options. End: "Direct import — 1 year warranty — COD Nepal." WhatsApp number.
📱 Phone tip: Film watch in slow motion (120fps if your phone supports). Add "Rs 999" text overlay in CapCut/InShot at the 2-second mark — time it to explode on screen. Red "Rs 4,000 🚫" crossed out with green "Rs 999 ✅" is a proven click magnet.
🥉 Hook 3 — Gift/Couple Moment (Highest Emotional Response)
What to film (0–3 sec): One hand passing a watch (or gift box) to another person's hand → both people put watches on → smile and hold hands showing both watches.

Nepali text overlay: "परफेक्ट गिफ्ट प्रियजनलाई!" (The perfect gift for your loved one!)

Continue: Show box, packaging, "Gents + Ladies set Rs 1,999." End: "Gift wrapping FREE. COD available. WhatsApp: [NUMBER]."

Best timing: Run this hook specifically before Dashain, Tihar, Valentine's Day — emotional triggers are 3x stronger during festival season.
📱 Phone tip: Film with a friend or partner. Natural sunlight. Keep it genuine and unscripted — the reaction when seeing the watches together should be real. Nepali buyers respond most to authenticity and real emotion, not studio-perfect production.
