### Nepal Price Psychology — Critical Rules

**Always Use Odd Pricing:**
- ❌ Rs 900 → ✅ Rs 899
- ❌ Rs 1,000 → ✅ Rs 999
- ❌ Rs 1,500 → ✅ Rs 1,499
- ❌ Rs 2,000 → ✅ Rs 1,999

**Framings That Kill Price Objections:**
- "Direct import — no middleman"
- "Direct factory price"
- "Premium build, Nepal price"
- "Bazarma Rs 4,000 → Hamro Rs 1,499"
- "1 year warranty included"
- "Cash on delivery — try first"

**Words That Never Work in Nepal:**
- ❌ "Cheap" (implies poor quality)
- ❌ "Replica" or "Copy"
- ❌ Hide price ("DM for price")
- ❌ "Limited time" without real deadline
- ❌ English-only posts to local audience

## 6. VIDEO HOOKS — 3 Proven Scripts

### Hook 1 — Wrist Sparkle/Transformation (Best Performer)
**0–3 sec:** Bare wrist → slide watch on slowly → twist wrist to catch light.
**Nepali text overlay:** "यो घडी लगाएपछि स्टाइल एकदम फरक पर्छ!"
**3–15 sec:** Show watch from multiple angles. Price reveal at 8 sec: "मात्र Rs [PRICE] मा". End: WhatsApp number + "COD Available."
**Phone tip:** Hold hand steady in sunlight near a window. Slow rotate to catch light sparkle on dial/strap. Film in 1080p. If bangle watch, this hook is 10x more powerful.

### Hook 2 — Price Pop Reveal (Drives Immediate Clicks)
**0–3 sec:** Close-up of watch face in hand → flip over showing the dial → price text explodes on screen.
**On-screen text:** "Bazarma Rs 4,000+ 🚫" (cross out) → "Hamro Price: Rs 999 ✅"
**Nepali text:** "मात्र Rs ९९९ मा premium look!"
**Continue:** Show 3 colour options. End: "Direct import — 1 year warranty — COD Nepal."
**Phone tip:** Film in slow motion (120fps). Add price text overlay in CapCut/InShot at the 2-second mark. Red "Rs 4,000 🚫" crossed out with green "Rs 999 ✅" is a proven click magnet.

### Hook 3 — Gift/Couple Moment (Highest Emotional Response)
**0–3 sec:** One hand passing a watch (or gift box) to another person's hand → both put watches on → smile and hold hands showing both watches.
**Nepali text overlay:** "परफेक्ट गिफ्ट प्रियजनलाई!"
**Continue:** Show box, packaging, "Gents + Ladies set Rs 1,999." End: "Gift wrapping FREE. COD available. WhatsApp: [NUMBER]."
**Best timing:** Run before Dashain, Tihar, Valentine's Day — emotional triggers are 3x stronger during festival season.
**Phone tip:** Film with a friend or partner. Natural sunlight. Keep it genuine — Nepali buyers respond most to authenticity.
