## Tiered Pricing Strategy

| Tier | Range | Role | Framing |
|------|-------|------|---------|
| Entry | Rs 500–900 | Traffic hook, organic seller, add-on | "कम बजेटमा स्मार्ट लुक" |
| Mid-range | Rs 1,000–1,800 | Core conversion engine — most ad budget | "Premium look, pocket-friendly" |
| Premium | Rs 2,000+ | AOV and margin driver | "Executive finish" — add value, don't discount |

**The hard economics:**
- Rs 900 watch → Rs 450 gross profit (too tight for cold ads)
- Rs 1,500 watch → Rs 750 gross profit
- Rs 2,300 watch → Rs 1,150 gross profit

**Rule:** Entry = hook. Mid = closer. Premium/bundles = scale.
